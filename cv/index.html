Profile

QA analyst specialised in factoring and receivables finance, with nearly five years inside the software that banks and factoring companies rely on every day. At Codix, I test iMX across the full business chain: third parties and screening, contracts and limits, invoicing and funding, payments and bank interfaces, allocation, collections, accounting and nightly batches. I read business rules like a business analyst and test them like an engineer, with SQL, application logs, REST APIs and ISO 20022 files, from DEV through to UAT. I make sure every release that reaches a client is one they can trust. I am also the dedicated tester of an AI solution, and I work in English, French and Arabic.

Experience

  1. Current

    QA Analyst, iMX Factoring Solutions · Codix

    2025–today

    iMX, factoring and receivables finance software for banks and factoring companies

    Business areas tested

    • Third parties and onboarding: creation of individuals and companies and their roles (client, debtor, guarantor and others), with sanctions and compliance screening of each party.
    • Contracts: contract set-up for each factoring product (recourse and non-recourse, disclosed and undisclosed, reverse factoring), amendments, renewals and closure.
    • Limits and credit risk: debtor limit requests through the approval workflow (approval, partial approval, refusal, expiry, renewal), limit consumption, over-limit and concentration cases, and their effect on funding.
    • Portfolio management: client and debtor portfolios, their outstanding balances, risk exposure and transfers between portfolios.
    • Invoicing and assignment: invoice and credit note intake by screen and file import, eligibility rules, ineligible and rejected items, and invoicing of the factor's own fees and commissions.
    • Funding: availability, advance rate and reserve, funding requests and payment orders, cut-off times, with fee and interest calculations checked against contract terms at edge cases (period end, rate change, minimum fee).
    • Payments and bank interfaces: outgoing SEPA and cross-border payments in ISO 20022 XML and incoming MT940 and CAMT.053 bank statements, traced from the bank file to the client account.
    • Multi-currency: invoices, funding and payments in several currencies, exchange rates and currency conversion on balances.
    • Financial items: creation, life cycle and balances of the financial items behind every invoice, payment, fee and adjustment.
    • Allocation: allocating debtor payments to invoices, including partial, over- and underpayments, splits, unidentified payments, reallocations, reversals and write-offs.
    • Collections and disputes: dunning cycles, overdue tracking and ageing, recourse on unpaid items, and disputes from opening to resolution, with their effect on eligibility and funding.
    • Accounting: the accounting impact of each operation (assignment, funding, allocation, fees, write-off), with client and debtor balances checked in SQL.
    • Batches and end-of-day runs: interest accrual, ageing, limit expiry, dunning and file runs, verified in the database and logs, including month ends, value dates and reruns after a failure.
    • APIs and web services: REST API and web service testing with Postman, checking that API calls create the same records and accounting as the screens.
    • Reporting and documents: client and debtor statements, notices, letters and operational reports, reconciled with the database.
    • User rights: roles, permissions, four-eyes approvals and the audit trail.
    • Configuration: the same flows checked under client- and country-specific parameter settings.

    How I work

    • Requirements: analysing business requirements and specifications, finding gaps and contradictions before development, and turning them into test scenarios and acceptance criteria with business experts and client teams.
    • Test design: positive, negative and boundary cases, end-to-end scenarios across the business chain, and the test data to run them.
    • Environments and releases: test execution across DEV, VAL and UAT, smoke tests, retests of each fix, and full regression and non-regression campaigns before each release.
    • Test management and defects: test cases and campaigns in Jira and Xray, evidence-based defect reports (steps, expected and actual results, SQL results, log extracts) and follow-up with developers until the fix is confirmed.
    • Investigation: root-cause analysis with SQL queries and application logs.

    AI testing

    • Dedicated tester of an AI solution: evaluating its answers for accuracy, consistency, made-up facts and risky cases.
    • Designing test questions with expected answers, and reporting quality issues to the team.
