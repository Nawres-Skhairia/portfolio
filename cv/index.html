Profile

Functional QA & Business Analyst with nearly five years' experience at Codix on iMX, an enterprise receivables finance platform used by banks and factoring companies. Functional and technical QA across the full factoring lifecycle: onboarding, individuals and roles with compliance screening, contracts and credit limits, invoicing and funding, payments and banking interfaces (SEPA, cross-border, ISO 20022, MT940, CAMT.053), cash allocation, collections, accounting and night jobs.
