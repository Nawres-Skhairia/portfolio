Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is my personal website. In short: it uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't ask you for any information.

Who is responsible

Nawres Skhairia, based in Bulgaria. You can reach me at skhairiyanawres@gmail.com.

When you visit

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Like any web host, Vercel briefly records technical information needed to deliver the pages and keep them secure, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. I don't use this information to identify you. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel processes data under its own privacy policy, including safeguards for transfers outside the EU.

Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so no other company is contacted when you browse it.

When you email me

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply to you. I keep them only as long as our conversation needs, and I delete them if you ask.

The tools on this site

The project tools run entirely in your browser. Files you open and anything you type stay on your device and are never sent to me or anyone else. Some tools save your sample data in your browser's local storage so it's still there next time. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is kept in your browser only until you close the tab. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Payment File Check and Test File Maker work with payment files, which may contain names and bank account numbers, inside your browser tab only: they are never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and are forgotten when you close the tab.

MT to MX Converter works the same way: a message you paste or open is converted and checked inside your browser tab only, is never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and is forgotten when you close the tab. Please use made-up messages only.

Payment Matcher works the same way: a bank statement and an invoice list you open are checked and matched inside your browser tab only, are never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and are forgotten when you close the tab. The audit trail exists only in the tab until you export it yourself. Please use made-up files only.

Your rights

This site doesn't collect or keep personal data. If you have emailed me, you can ask me at any time to delete our conversation, and I will. For general questions about data protection in Bulgaria, the national authority is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).
