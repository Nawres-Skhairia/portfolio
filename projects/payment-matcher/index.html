        Audit trail

        Every line carries the fingerprint (SHA-256) of the line before it. A change to an exported trail can be found by comparing it with the final fingerprint printed in the report.

        #TimeActorActionEntriesInvoicesRulePaidDifferenceReason or detailFingerprint

        Honest limit: without a server or a signing key, someone could rebuild the whole chain after an edit. The check proves a trail is unchanged only against the final fingerprint printed in the report, so keep the report.
