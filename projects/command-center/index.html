Command Center

Every project, risk and deadline on one screen.

When a team runs many projects at once, the real picture is spread across plans, task boards and status emails. Command Center brings it together and tells you, with figures you can check, which project needs attention and why.

  • Health score for each project, with the reasons behind it
  • Dependencies that show what blocks what, and the critical path
  • Risk register with an impact and probability matrix
  • Reports ready to print or export, from the live figures
