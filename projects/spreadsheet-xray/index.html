Spreadsheet X-ray

Open that one Excel file everyone relies on, and X-ray explains it in plain words: how the numbers are calculated, the rules hidden in the formulas, and the cells that could quietly go wrong. Ask it questions in French or English.

try it in one click

A deliberately messy sales commission workbook, the kind that ends up named FINAL_v3 (2).xlsx.

Your file is read inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
