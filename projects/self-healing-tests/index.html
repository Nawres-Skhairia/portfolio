        Under the hood

        How the healer finds a moved element

        Every test target was recorded once on the original page: its kind (button, text box…), its words (button text or label), the section it sits in, its test id and its rough place. When the test id is gone, the healer scores every element on the new page against that record:

          The score is a match score, not a probability. Under the healer gives up and asks for a person.

          Why a repair can be refused

          A proposal changes nothing on its own. The robot runs the whole test again on a fresh page. Green: the repair is kept. Still red: the healer tries the next best element. If nothing turns green and every match was clear, the page is wrong, not the test: real bug.
