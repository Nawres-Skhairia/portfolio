    How it checks a file

    1. Safety gateUp to 10 MB. Files with a DOCTYPE or ENTITY are refused, which blocks the classic XML attacks (XXE, "billion laughs").
    2. 1 · Well-formed XMLEvery tag opened is closed, in the right order.
    3. 2 · Official schemaThe unchanged ISO 20022 pain.001.001.09 schema, checked by libxml2 compiled to WebAssembly, in a separate worker that is stopped after 20 seconds.
    4. 3 · SEPA rulesWhat the schema cannot see: sums and counts, IBAN check digits, 2 decimals in euro, character set, dates. Amounts are added exactly, never as floating point.
    5. ReportEach finding at its line, with its path, what was expected and found, and how to fix it. Export as HTML, JSON or JUnit for a build pipeline.

    Not a certified validator. It checks the official schema and the rules listed below; your bank may apply more. Nothing you open here is sent anywhere: the file is read in this browser tab and forgotten when you close it.
