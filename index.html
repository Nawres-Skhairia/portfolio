Behind the tools

I'm Nawres. I notice the small frictions in everyday work: the spreadsheet nobody dares to touch, the follow-up that slips through, the calculation done by hand every month. Then I build the tool that fixes it.

AI lets me move fast. The part I never hand over is the judgement: checking every number, sweating the small details, and testing each tool the way a real user would.

This is my little workshop: side projects, made for the pleasure of building something useful. More are on the way.
